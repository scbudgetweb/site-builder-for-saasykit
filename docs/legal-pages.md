# Legal pages

**Where:** Admin › Website › Legal pages
**Who can use it:** administrators with the *update settings* permission

Edit your **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**. Each has its own tab. Your app may add more legal pages, such as a cookie policy, which appear as extra tabs.

## Editing a page

Each tab opens with the page's current text. Until you've saved your own, that's the **standard template**: general wording supplied with the site. Check it against how your site actually works, and take legal advice if you're unsure.

The editor has bold, italic, underline, links, headings, paragraphs, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes, dividing lines, *clear formatting*, undo and redo.

Click **Save changes** to save every tab at once. The public page then shows **Last updated** with the date you last changed it. Saving a tab without changing it doesn't change its date.

## Merge tags

Type `{{` in the editor to insert a **merge tag**. It's replaced with the real value when the page is shown, so the text stays right if the details change:

| Merge tag | Becomes |
|---|---|
| **Site name** | The site's name |
| **Site URL** | The site's web address |
| **Support email** | The support email address from the app's settings |
| **Privacy Policy URL** / **Terms of Service URL** | The address of that page (and of any other legal page your app adds) |

## Going back to the template

To go back to the standard template, select all the text in a tab, delete it, and save. The page shows the template again, without a *Last updated* date, and the tab reopens with the template text.

## Good to know

- **Save changes** saves every tab. A tab you never touched is saved as a copy of the template, with its own *Last updated* date.
- The template's wording is the app's, with your site's name and address filled in when the tab first opens.
- For safety, anything that isn't ordinary formatted text (such as scripts) is removed when the page is shown.
