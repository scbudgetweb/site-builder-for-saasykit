# Pages

**Where:** Admin › Website › Pages
**Who can use it:** administrators with the page permissions (*view*, *create*, *update* and *delete pages*). The admin role has them all.

Build pages for your website from ready-made **blocks** (banners, text, images, features, testimonials, videos and more), with no code. Every block follows your [Branding](branding.md), so pages match the site's colours and fonts, and they work on phones.

A page can also be shown **in place of** a built-in page, such as the home page.

## Creating a page

1. Go to *Website › Pages* and click **New page**.
2. Enter a **Title**. The **URL** fills in from the title (*About us* becomes `/about-us`); you can change it.
3. Click **Create**. You're taken to the page editor.
4. On the **Content** tab, click **Add block**, choose a block and fill it in. Repeat for each block.
5. When the page is ready, set **Status** to *Published* on the **Settings** tab, then **Save**.

**Preview**, at the top of the editor, shows the page as visitors will see it, including drafts. The preview link works for an hour, and only for people who have it.

## The editor

### Content tab

Each block shows as a card with its name, heading, a short extract and its first image. Small tags show how it's styled (for example *Gradient*, *Panel*, *Border*, *Shadow* or an anchor such as *#pricing*), so you can see a page's styling without opening every block.

- **Click a card** to edit the block in a panel that slides in from the right.
- The panel's footer has **Save page** and **Preview** as well as *Save changes*:
  - **Save page** saves the whole page without closing the panel, even for a block inside a section. If something needs fixing, the message shows in the panel and nothing is saved.
  - **Preview** opens the page in its own tab, which reloads each time you save.
  - **Save changes** keeps your changes and closes the panel. The page itself is saved when you click **Save page** or the page's **Save changes** button.
- **Drag** cards, or use the arrows, to reorder them. The card's buttons **copy** or **delete** a block (you're asked to confirm), **save it to the [block library](block-library.md)**, or, on a linked library block, **make an editable copy**.
- **Add block**, at the bottom or between blocks, opens the block picker.

If a block has a problem when you save, a message names it, for example *Block 2 (Section) › Row 1 › Block 2 (Call to action): The heading field is required.*

### Settings tab

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The page's name, shown in its banner and in browser tabs |
| **URL** | The address, for example `/about-us`. Lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens only. Addresses the site already uses (such as `/blog`) can't be chosen |
| **Status** | *Draft* pages are hidden from visitors; *Published* pages are live |
| **Replaces built-in page** | Shows this page instead of a built-in page, such as the home page (see below) |
| **Show the standard page banner** | Adds the inner-page banner with the page title at the top. It isn't shown when the page starts with its own Hero or Banner |

### SEO tab

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title for search engines** | The title in search results and browser tabs. Leave blank to use the page title |
| **Description for search engines** | The short description under the title in search results |
| **Share image** | The picture shown when the page is shared on social media (1200 × 630 pixels works best). Leave blank to use the share image from Branding |
| **Hide from search engines** | Asks search engines not to list the page, and leaves it out of the sitemap |

Published pages are added to the site's sitemap automatically.

## Replacing a built-in page

Choose a page in **Replaces built-in page** to show your page at that address instead, for example your own home page. Only one page can replace each built-in page. The page's own URL then sends visitors to the built-in address.

Some built-in pages need a particular block to keep working. If yours is missing it, a warning says which block to add. While a page is a draft or in the bin, the built-in page shows as normal.

## Blocks

### Layout and content

| Block | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Section (rows of blocks)** | One styled area holding **rows** of blocks, each row stacked or in columns. See [Sections and rows](#sections-and-rows) |
| **Hero** | The big opening banner of a landing page, taller than a Banner, with a larger heading |
| **Banner** | A full-width banner with an optional small label, a heading, a subtitle and up to two buttons. Its background can match the site's banners or use your own image with an overlay of one colour or a gradient |
| **Text** | Formatted text: headings, lists, links, quotes and tables, with up to two buttons above or below it |
| **Image + text** | A heading, text and buttons with an image on the left or right (side by side on larger screens), or above or below them. The image is optional |
| **Image** | One image with a caption, an optional link, and up to two buttons |
| **Features** | A grid of features or benefits, each with an [icon](#icons), title, text and link |
| **Image cards** | Cards with an image or an icon, a title, short text and a button. With more cards than columns they become a carousel, which can move on by itself |
| **Steps (how it works)** | Numbered steps, for example *Choose a date → Book → Enjoy*. Each step can show an icon instead of its number |
| **Stats** | Big numbers with labels. Type your own, or show live figures from the app. They can count up as visitors scroll to them |
| **Call to action** | A heading, a line of text and buttons |
| **Buttons** | One to three buttons |
| **Testimonials** | Quotes with the person's name, role and photo |
| **Team** | People with a photo, role, short bio and links |
| **FAQ** | Questions that open to show their answers. Search engines are told the page has FAQs |
| **Tabs** | Content split into tabs |
| **Saved block (library)** | A block or section from the [block library](block-library.md), linked so it updates whenever the library block changes |

### Media

| Block | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Gallery** | A grid of images that open larger when clicked |
| **Video** | A YouTube, Vimeo or Adilo video. See [Videos and privacy](#videos-and-privacy) |
| **Logo strip** | Partner or sponsor logos: a still row, a scrolling loop or a carousel. Moving logos have a pause button, and stay still for visitors who turn off animations |
| **Social follow** | Icons linking to your social profiles, from the app's general settings (web addresses only) |
| **Divider / spacer** | A line (with an optional label) or empty space between blocks |

Your app may add blocks of its own. They appear at the end of the block picker.

## Sections and rows

A **Section** is one styled area, such as a grey band or a coloured panel, holding one or more **rows** of blocks.

- **Each row has its own layout:** stacked (one under another), 2, 3 or 4 equal columns, *narrow + wide* (⅓ + ⅔) or *wide + narrow* (⅔ + ⅓). Columns sit side by side on tablets and computers, and stack on phones.
- **One block per column.** A row's blocks fill its columns in order. To put two blocks in one column, add another row.
- **Line up blocks at the:** *Top*, *Middle*, *Bottom* or *Same height*. *Same height* stretches the blocks in a row to match, and lines up their buttons at the bottom.
- **Last block first on phones** shows the row's last block on top on phones, for example so an image on the right appears above its text.
- **Row style** (collapsed under each row) adds a border, shadow, rounded corners and space inside around just that row.
- **Add row** adds another row; rows can be reordered, copied and collapsed.
- The section's **Layout** tab sets the space between rows and columns.

Blocks inside a section take its text colour and heading decoration, unless you give them their own. Sections can't contain a Hero, a Banner or another Section.

## Block settings

Each block's panel is split into tabs, always in this order:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Content** (**Rows** for a section) | The words, images, items and buttons |
| **Layout** | How it sits on the page: columns or alignment, content width, space above and below, heading alignment, and the background area (full width or a panel) |
| **Style** | How it looks: the block's own look (for example its cards or images), its background and text colour, heading decoration and sizes, and its border and shadow |
| **Advanced** | Anchor ID and CSS classes |

Only settings that do something for that block are shown. For example, heading settings don't appear on a Text block, and a Divider has nothing to style.

### Layout

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Content width** | Narrow (for reading), Contained, Wide or Full width |
| **Space above and below** | None, Small, Medium or Large |
| **Heading alignment** | The block's usual alignment, Left or Centre |
| **Background area** | *Full width*, or *Behind the content, as a panel*: a box with its own corners, which can **overlap the block above** |

### Style

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Background** | None, Light grey, Site colour (light tint, normal or dark), Dark, a custom colour, a gradient of two or three colours, or an image with a one-colour or gradient overlay |
| **Text colour** | Automatic (from the background), Dark or Light |
| **Heading decoration** | Site style (from Branding), None, a short bar under the heading, a full-width line under it, or a short bar above it |
| **Heading size** / **Text size** | From Small to Huge, or Standard |
| **Border & shadow** | See below |

#### Borders and shadows

The same border and shadow settings appear on blocks, section rows, images, cards and buttons:

- **Border:** none, solid, dashed, dotted or double, with a **width** in pixels, a **colour** (blank gives a soft line in the text colour) and which **sides** (all, top, bottom, top and bottom, left, right, or left and right).
- **Shadow:** none, *Site style* (from Branding), Soft, Medium, Strong, or **Custom**, where you set how far it falls across and down, the blur, the spread, the colour and its strength.

On a block, the border and shadow go round its panel when it has one, and round the whole block otherwise. *Automatic* gives a panel the site's shadow and leaves other blocks without one. Images (in Image, Image + text, Image cards and Gallery) have their own corners, border and shadow on the Style tab. Cards have a border line and width as well as their colours. Buttons with custom colours can have a border line and width, and any button can have a shadow.

### Advanced

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Anchor ID** | A name for linking straight to the block, for example `pricing` for `/about-us#pricing` |
| **CSS classes** | Extra classes for a developer's custom CSS (Branding › Custom CSS). `fixed` and `sticky` aren't allowed, so a block can't pin itself over the page |

## Buttons

Buttons appear in many blocks. Each one has a **label**, a **link** (a full web address, a page on this site starting with `/`, a part of the page starting with `#`, an email address or a phone number), an optional [icon](#icons), whether it **opens in a new tab**, a **style**, a **width** and its position in the block.

**Style** is one of the four button styles from Branding, *Automatic* (the right style for the background the button sits on), or **custom colours** for this button only.

## Icons

Features, steps, stats, image cards and buttons can show an icon. Type to search over 1,600 icons using everyday words, such as *calendar*, *map* or *trophy*. Add *filled* or *logo* to find those styles. Icons are stored on the site, so nothing is loaded from another server.

## Videos and privacy

Until a visitor accepts marketing cookies, a video shows a still picture with a play button, and nothing loads from YouTube, Vimeo or Adilo until it's clicked. Once marketing cookies are allowed, the player shows straight away. YouTube videos always use its privacy-enhanced mode.

## Images

Images can be JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB. An Image or Image + text block needs **Alternative text**: a short description of the picture for people using screen readers, and for search engines.

An image you remove from a page is deleted from storage once nothing uses it any more. That includes other pages, the block library and the page's kept [revisions](revisions.md).

## Duplicating, deleting and restoring pages

**Duplicate** (in the list of pages) makes a draft copy, titled *… (copy)*, with its own address. It never replaces a built-in page until you choose one.

**Delete** moves a page to the bin. It stops showing on the site, and any built-in page it replaced shows again. A page in the bin can still be edited, but its status and the built-in page it replaces can't be changed until it's restored. Use the list's **Deleted records** filter to find it, then **Restore** it or **Delete permanently**. Deleting permanently also removes its revisions and any images nothing else uses, and can't be undone.
