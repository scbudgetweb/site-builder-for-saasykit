# Pages

**Where:** Admin › Website › Pages
**Who can use it:** administrators with the page permissions (*view*, *create*, *update* and *delete pages*). The admin role has them all.

Build pages for your website from ready-made **blocks** (banners, text, images, features, testimonials, videos and more), with no code. Every block follows your [Branding](branding.md), so pages match the site's colours and fonts, and they work on phones.

A page can also be shown **in place of** a built-in page, such as the home page.

## Creating a page

1. Go to *Website › Pages* and click **New page**.
2. Enter a **Title**. The **Web address** fills in from the title (*About us* becomes `/about-us`); you can change it.
3. Click **Create**. You're taken to the page editor.
4. On the **Content** tab, click **Add block**, choose a block and fill it in. Repeat for each block.
5. When the page is ready, set **Status** to *Published* on the **Settings** tab, then **Save**.

**Preview**, at the top of the editor, shows the page as visitors will see it, including drafts. The preview link works for an hour, and only for people who have it.

## The visual builder

Clicking a page in the list opens the **visual builder**: the page as visitors see it, full screen, at **Phone**, **Tablet** or **Desktop** width.

- **Point at a block** to outline it, and **click** it to open its settings. They appear in a floating panel that you can drag by its title and resize from its corner, and it opens where you left it.
- **The page updates as you edit**, before you save. *Cancel* puts it back. A newly uploaded image shows once you save.
- **Your changes are saved as you go** into your own draft, and visitors see nothing until you press **Publish** (**Save** for a page that's still a draft). **Discard changes** goes back to the page as it is. If someone else saves the page while you're working, Publish asks before replacing their changes.
- **Add blocks** with the round **+** at the top and bottom of the block you're pointing at, or **Add a block above / below** in its right-click menu. Choose the block, and its settings open. Inside a Section's column, only blocks that fit in one are offered. Click an empty column, or an empty page, to add the first block there.
- **Add rows** with the green **+ Add row** at the bottom of a Section you're pointing at, or the green **+** under a row (a new row below it). First choose the row's columns from the pictures (one column, two to five equal columns, or uneven ones such as ⅓ + ⅔); the row arrives with that many empty columns. The + buttons are colour-coded: green adds a row, grey adds a block.
- **Move things** by dragging: point at a block and drag its **Move** handle, or a row's **⠿** handle (pointing at a row outlines it in green, with its ⚙, ⠿ and + just outside its left edge). A blue line shows where it'll land: above or below a block, or into a column (a row: into a Section). The page scrolls when you drag near its top or bottom. Or use the right-click menu: **Move up / down** and **Move to the column on the left / right**.
- **The right-click menu** also has **Duplicate** and **Delete** (no question asked: Ctrl+Z puts it back). Inside a Section, it has **Row settings** (layout, line-up, row style), **Add a row below**, **Duplicate row**, **Move row up / down**, **Delete row**, and **Column … settings** (background, line-up, space, border). On a Section itself: **Add a row**. Every part also has **Import…** and **Export** (see [Import and export](#import-and-export)). A long menu scrolls.
- A block with nothing to show yet (just added, say) shows as a dashed box on the canvas, so you can click it and fill it in. Visitors never see it.
- **Layers** (the stack in the toolbar) opens a panel beside the canvas listing everything on the page as a tree: blocks, Sections, their rows and columns, and the blocks in each column. Each shows its admin label (or what it is) and a few words of its content. The panel stays open next time, in this browser.
  - **Click** one to scroll to it and open its settings; **point** at one to outline it on the canvas.
  - The **arrows** fold a Section, row or column away.
  - **Drag** a block or row to move it: a block above or below another, or into a column; a row above or below another, or into a Section.
  - The **eye** hides one on every screen (Design › Visibility › Hide on), so visitors don't see it; it still shows, faded, in the builder. A faded eye means it's hidden on some screens only. Click again to show it everywhere.
  - **Double-click** one, or use the **pencil**, to rename it: its admin label, only seen in the builder. Enter saves, Esc doesn't; leave it blank for its usual name.

  Each change goes into your draft as any other, so undo takes it back.
- **Undo and redo** with the arrows in the toolbar, or **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y** (**⌘Z** and **⇧⌘Z** on a Mac). The last 30 changes are kept, until you publish or discard. While you're typing in a panel, those keys undo your typing instead.
- **Revisions** (the clock in the toolbar) lists each saved version, with Preview and Restore. Restore puts that version's blocks into your draft, so you can check it before publishing, and undo takes it back. Its title and search settings are restored in *Edit as a form*.
- **Right-click a block** for its menu:
  - **Copy style** copies all of its style, or one part: background, spacing & size, heading text, body text, links, borders, buttons or effects.
  - **Paste style** puts it on another block (on this page or another), replacing that part of its style. Its content stays as it is.
  - **Give all … blocks this style** copies this block's style, or one part of it, to every other block of the same type on the page, including those in Sections. Handy after restyling one heading.

  Each paste is one change, so Ctrl+Z (⌘Z) takes it back. "All styles" includes a block's own settings (such as its cards' look) only between blocks of the same type. A linked library block is styled in the block library.
- **Edit as a form** opens the same page in the form editor below, where the page's settings (address, status, search and sharing) are. Your draft stays in the visual builder until you publish or discard it.

## The editor

### Content tab

Each block shows as a card with its name, heading, a short extract and its first image. Small tags show how it's styled (for example *Gradient*, *Panel*, *Border*, *Shadow* or an anchor such as *#pricing*), so you can see a page's styling without opening every block.

- **Click a card** to edit the block in a panel that slides in from the right.
- The panel's footer has **Save page** and **Preview** as well as *Save changes*:
  - **Save page** saves the whole page without closing the panel, even for a block inside a section. If something needs fixing, the message shows in the panel and nothing is saved.
  - **Preview** opens the page in its own tab, which reloads each time you save.
  - **Save changes** keeps your changes and closes the panel. The page itself is saved when you click **Save page** or the page's **Save changes** button.
- **Drag** cards, or use the arrows, to reorder them. The card's buttons **copy** or **delete** a block (you're asked to confirm), **save it to the [block library](block-library.md)**, or, on a linked library block, **make an editable copy**.
- **Add block**, at the bottom or between blocks, opens the block picker.

If a block has a problem when you save, a message names it, for example *Block 2 (Section) › Row 1 › Column 2 › Block 1 (Call to action): The heading field is required.*

### Settings tab

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The page's name, shown in its banner and in browser tabs |
| **Web address** | The address, for example `/about-us`. Lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens only. Addresses the site already uses (such as `/blog`) can't be chosen |
| **Status** | *Draft* pages are hidden from visitors; *Published* pages are live |
| **Replaces built-in page** | Shows this page instead of a built-in page, such as the home page (see below) |
| **Show the standard page banner** | Adds the inner-page banner with the page title at the top. It isn't shown when the page starts with its own Hero or Banner |

### Search & sharing tab

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title for search engines** | The title in search results and browser tabs. Leave blank to use the page title |
| **Description for search engines** | The short description under the title in search results |
| **Share image** | The picture shown when the page is shared on social media (1200 × 630 pixels works best). Leave blank to use the share image from Branding |
| **Hide from search engines** | Asks search engines not to list the page, and leaves it out of the sitemap |

Published pages are added to the site's sitemap automatically.

## Replacing a built-in page

Choose a page in **Replaces built-in page** to show your page at that address instead, for example your own home page. Only one page can replace each built-in page. The page's own web address then sends visitors to the built-in address.

Some built-in pages need a particular block to keep working. If yours is missing it, a warning says which block to add. While a page is a draft or in the bin, the built-in page shows as normal.

It counts in the visitor's browser, so a cached page is never out of date. Each unit is an item: Design › Items gives them a box (e.g. white tiles), and Number text and Label text style them |

Style: the colour (blank: the site colour), the track (the unfilled part), the size and the ring thickness; an item can have its own colour. They fill up as they scroll into view (turn off *Fill up when scrolled into view* to show them full at once), and the % sign can be turned off. Design › Items lays them out and styles the numbers and labels |
| **Bar counters** | Percentages as bars, each with its label and number above it (up to 12), with the same colours, % sign and filling up as Circle counters, and a choice of bar height |
| **Pricing table** | Up to four plans side by side, each a card with a name, a short description, a price (a number gets the currency symbol in front; a word such as *Free* stays as it is), its period (*per month*), feature lines and a button. In **Features**, put one per line, and start a line with - for something not included (shown crossed and dimmed). **Featured** makes a plan stand out with a ring, and its **Badge** (e.g. *Most popular*) sits on its edge. **Prices** can be *One price* (as above) or *Monthly and annual*: each plan's **Price** is then its price per month when billed annually, and its **Monthly price** is worked out from the **Annual discount** (149 at 20% off is 186.25, shown faint in the field). Fill it in to round it or set your own. Visitors switch between **Annually** (with *Save 20%*) and **Monthly**; choose which is **Shown first**, the switch's wording, and the lines under each price (*:total billed annually*, where :total is the price for the year, and *Billed monthly*). Turn on **Price by company size** to add **Sizes** (as many as you need), each with a price for every plan in the plans' order; visitors choose their size from a list (**List label**, *Company size*). A blank price shows *Let's talk* and a line under it, both yours to change. **Add the visitor's choice to the plan buttons' links** adds `?size=…&billing=annual` (or `monthly`) to buttons that link to a page, so a Contact form there can fill them in (its fields' **Filled in from the link**: `size` and `billing`). Where the app has SaaSykit's plans, **Plans › Your SaaSykit plans** shows the live plans instead, with their sign-up links and their own Monthly and Yearly switch, as the home page does; a page showing them isn't cached. | **Call to action** | A heading, a line of text and buttons |

Its **Fields** are a list you set: add a field, choose its **type** (Name, Email, Phone, Text, Long text, Dropdown, Radio buttons or Checkboxes, for more than one answer), its **label** and **placeholder**, whether it's **required**, **full or half width** (two half-width fields sit side by side, and stack on phones), its **choices** (one per line), an optional line of **help text**, and **Filled in from the link**: a name such as `size`, so `/contact?size=21-50` fills the field in (a choice is picked when it matches, ignoring case, or when it's the only one starting with it, so `annual` picks *Annually*). Drag to reorder. A new form has Name, Email and Message. Every form has one **Email** field, which replying uses: move it or rename it, but it can't be removed. Answers to choices must be one of them. An optional tick box asks them to agree to their details being kept (linking to your Privacy Policy). Every message is kept in **Website › Contact messages**. With **Email messages** on (as standard), it's emailed too: to the form's **Deliver to** address, or, left blank, the Support Email in General settings › Application. Replying to the email answers the visitor. Turn it off to keep messages in Contact messages only. After sending: a thank-you message (yours, or the standard one) or a page to go to. ## The block library in the visual builder

A block library item opens in the visual builder too: **Visual builder** on its row in the library, or at the top of its form. It shows on its own, in the site's look, and works like a page's, except:

- an item is one block (often a Section), so you add and move blocks inside it, not around it;
- **Save** replaces Publish, and says first how many pages use the item: they all change when you save;
- there are no revisions.

## Import and export

Any page, template or library block, or one part of it, can be saved as a file and brought into another page, on this site or another one with the site builder.

**Export** downloads a `.json` file named after the page, the part (if you renamed it in Layers) and the date, e.g. `about-us-page-2026-11-03.json`:

- **The whole page:** **Export** in the visual builder's toolbar, at the top of *Edit as a form*, or on its row in the list of pages.
- **One part:** right-click a block, Section, row or column › **Export block / section / row / column**. In *Edit as a form*, each block card has **Export** too.

A page's file holds its blocks only: not its title, address, search settings or banner, and not the site's header and footer (templates have their own export, see the [Theme builder](theme-builder.md#import-and-export)). Linked library blocks and saved styles are written out in full, as they won't exist on another site. Images are saved as links to this site's uploads, so on another site they're left out (see below). The export is of your draft, so it's what you see in the builder.

**Import** puts a file's blocks into your draft, so nothing changes for visitors until you publish, and **Undo** takes it back:

- **Into the page:** **Import page** in the toolbar. **Replace the page's content** is on as standard, with a red warning: everything on the page is replaced. Turn it off to add the file's blocks at the bottom instead.
- **Next to a part:** right-click › **Import…** (on a row: *Import a row below…*; on a column: *Import into column…*). Where it goes:

  | Right-clicked on | A block or Section file | A row file | A column file |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | A block or Section on the page | after it | at the end of the Section | — |
  | A row | — | after it | — |
  | A column | at the end of the column (blocks that fit in a column) | — | its blocks, at the end of the column |
  | A block in a column | after it (blocks that fit in a column) | after its row | its blocks, after it |

  Where something can't go (—), the message says where it can.
- **In *Edit as a form*:** **Import** at the top replaces the page's blocks (or adds them at the end), and **Import after this** on a block card adds after it. Both change the form only, so press **Save** to keep them. Rows and columns are imported in the visual builder.

Every import is checked first, against the same rules as the editor. A file that isn't a site builder export, is from a newer version, has a block this site doesn't have, or has a setting that doesn't exist or isn't allowed, is refused, and nothing changes; the message lists the problems. An image that isn't uploaded on this site is left out instead, and listed after the import, so you can choose a new one. Files can be up to 5 MB.

## Sections and rows

A **Section** is one styled area, such as a grey band or a coloured panel, holding one or more **rows** of blocks.

**In the visual builder, each part has its own panel.** One is open at a time: opening another from the canvas saves the open one first (Undo takes it back).

- **Click a Section** to open its own settings (background, spacing, design options). Its rows are edited on the canvas.
- **A row:** hover over it and click the **⚙** at its top left, or right-click › Row settings. Its panel has the **Column structure**, a list of its **Columns**, and a **Background** (colour or gradient, and text colour), plus every design option.
- **A column:** click it in its row's Columns list (the row is saved first), or right-click › Column settings. A one-column row has one column, with its own settings too. Its panel has a back arrow to the row.
- **A block:** click it.
- **Rows and columns have a block's settings:** Background (colour, gradient or image, with an overlay), Link (the whole row or column clickable; its own buttons and links still work), Admin label, and on Advanced, CSS ID & classes. Each panel shows only what suits it: text settings (fonts, sizes, colours, heading decoration) are on blocks only; Dividers are on Sections only; and a column has no Sizing or margin, only padding, as it fills its share of the row.
- A row's background colours its text too, unless a column has its own.

**In Edit as a form**, the Section holds its rows in the form itself:

- **Each row has its own layout**, grouped in the list by how many columns it has: one column (blocks one under another), two to five equal columns, or uneven columns, such as *Narrow left, wide right (⅓ + ⅔)*, ¼ + ¾, 40% + 60%, or *Wide middle, thin sides (¼ + ½ + ¼)*. Columns sit side by side on tablets and computers, and stack on phones.
- **Each column holds its own blocks**, one under another. The row shows a box per column (*Column 1*, *Column 2*…), each with its own **Add block to column…** button. For example, put two Text blocks and an Image in column 1 and one large Image in column 2.
- **Move a block to another column** with the left and right arrows on its card. It goes to the end of that column. Drag a card to reorder the blocks within a column, and use the arrows on a column to swap whole columns.
- **Changing the layout keeps your blocks.** With fewer columns, the blocks from the columns that no longer fit move to the end of the last one; with more, the new columns start empty. An empty column keeps its place on the page.
- **Line up columns at the:** *Top*, *Middle*, *Bottom* or *Same height*. *Same height* stretches the columns in a row to match, and lines up their buttons at the bottom; in a column with several blocks, the last one stretches.
- **Space between columns** sets this row's own gap, from *None* (columns touch, e.g. two coloured halves) to *Large*. *Same as the section* uses the section's setting.
- **Last column first on phones** shows the row's last column on top on phones, for example so an image on the right appears above its text.
- **Side by side on phones too** stops the columns stacking on phones, for example a header's logo, menu and account links in one line.
- **Column settings** (collapsed in each column) give that column its own background (a preset, a custom colour or a gradient), text colour and vertical alignment (*Line up at the*). Its **More options** add space inside, rounded corners, a border and a shadow. A column with a background, border or shadow gets some space inside automatically, and a dark background makes the column's text light. Column settings apply when the row has columns side by side; a stacked row ignores them.
- **Colours show where you are**, in both builders: Sections are blue, rows green, columns purple and blocks grey, along the top of their cards and panels and on the canvas outlines.
- **Each row and column has a ⚙** (next to its move and delete buttons) that opens its settings: the same Row and Column settings panels as the visual builder, with Background, Link, Admin label, CSS ID & classes and the Design tab. The row's layout stays in the form.
- **Add row** adds another row; rows can be reordered, copied and collapsed. Each row is numbered (*Row 1 · Two equal columns: Text | Image*), and a block's panel is titled with where it is, e.g. *Section › Row 1 › Column 2 › Text*.
- The section's **Design › Layout** toggle sets the space between rows and columns.

Blocks inside a section take its text colour and heading decoration, unless you give them their own. Sections can't contain a Hero, a Banner or another Section.

## Block settings

Each block's panel has three tabs, **Content**, **Design** and **Advanced** (a Section's first tab is **Rows**). Each tab is a list of **toggles**, groups of settings that open with a click. Only the block's own content starts open.

| Tab | Toggles, in order |
|---|---|
| **Content** | The block's content (words, images, items and buttons) · **Link** · **Background** · **Admin label** |
| **Design** | The block's own settings (**Layout**, then its look, such as **Image** or **Cards**) · **Text** · **Heading text**, **Body text**, **Link text** · **Lists & quotes** · **Dividers** · **Buttons** · **Sizing** · **Spacing** · **Border** · **Box shadow** · **Filters** · **Transform** · **Animation** |
| **Advanced** | **CSS ID & classes** · **Conditions** · **Visibility** · **Transitions** · **Position** · **Scroll effects** |

Only settings that do something for that block are shown. For example, heading settings don't appear on a Text block, and a Banner has no Sizing. A **?** beside a setting explains it when you hover over it, and every button on a block card has a tooltip.

At the top of the panel:

- **Search options** finds a setting by name on every tab, and opens the toggles that have it.
- **Desktop, Tablet, Phone and Hover** choose which values the panel edits. In **Hover**, every setting that can change while the block is pointed at shows its hover value: sizes and spacing, corners and border widths, colours (text, headings, background, border), the shadow, transform (scale, lift, rotate, move), filters and opacity. Settings that can't change on hover are dimmed. Size fields also have their own Desktop, Tablet, Phone and Hover buttons, and a dot marks the ones with a value. Under **Transform**, *Hover changes* applies them to the whole block or to each item (the card, feature or testimonial being pointed at).
- **Preset** picks a saved style for this kind of block. A preset fills in every style setting the block leaves as it is, so the block's own settings always win, and changing a preset changes every block using it. A block with no choice uses the type's **default preset**, or pick *No preset*. People with the presets permission (*update settings* by default) can **Save as preset** (the block's style moves into the new preset), **Update preset** from this block, **Make default** and **Delete preset**. Content is never part of a preset.
- **Saved style** is the same for one group of settings, on any kind of block. It sits at the top of the Items (Cards, Numbers…), Heading text, Body text, Link text, Buttons, Background, Spacing, Border, Filters and Transform toggles. Save a look once, for example *Stat grid with dividers* or *Gold headings*, and pick it on any block with those settings. Changing the style changes every block using it. The block's own settings win, then its saved styles, then its preset; a preset can choose saved styles too. The same people can **Save as style** (the settings move into the style), **Update style** and **Delete style**. Copying a group's styles to another block keeps its saved style.
- **Dynamic content** (the `{x}` button) puts a tag into the text setting you last clicked: `{{ page_title }}`, `{{ site_name }}`, `{{ current_date }}`, `{{ current_year }}` and `{{ user_name }}` (the signed-in visitor's name). In a [Blog post template](theme-builder.md#blog-post-templates), the post's too: `{{ post_title }}`, `{{ post_excerpt }}`, `{{ post_date }}`, `{{ post_author }}` and `{{ post_category }}`; in a [Blog archive template](theme-builder.md#blog-archive-templates), `{{ archive_title }}`. Add a fallback for when there's no value: `{{ user_name | there }}`. Rich text has its own tags button, or type `{{`. Blocks with tags that change by visitor, time or page are drawn on each visit; the rest of the page stays cached.
- **Colours** can be see-through (drag the picker's transparency slider) or one of the **global colours** from Branding › Brand › Colours, shown as swatches under the picker.
- **Show only modified settings** (the funnel) hides the toggles you haven't changed. A toggle with changes has a dot by its name, and a **Reset** button that puts just that toggle back to its defaults.
- In the visual builder, **Snap to the left** and **Snap to the right** move the floating panel to the edge of the window, and **Expand** makes it almost full screen. Click Expand again to shrink it.

Row and column settings in the visual builder use the same layout: a row's **Column structure** on Content, a column's Background on Content, and on Design and Advanced the same design options a block has (fonts, sizing, spacing, borders, filters, transform, dividers, position…), applied to just that row or column.

### Content › Background and Admin label

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Background** | None, Light grey, Site colour (light tint, normal or dark), Dark, a custom colour, a gradient of two or three colours (straight, round or a sweep around a point), or an image with a one-colour or gradient overlay. For a gradient: straight or round. For an image: its size, which part stays in view, repeating, and *Stay still* |
| **Text colour** | Automatic (from the background), Dark or Light |
| **Background area** | *Full width*, or *Behind the content, as a panel*: a box with its own corners, border and shadow |
| **Pattern & shape** | A pattern over the background (dots, grid, lines, stripes, checks or waves) and a decorative shape (blob, arc, slant, circle or triangle), each with a colour and strength |
| **When empty** | Blocks that pull content in (Stats with live figures, and your app's own, e.g. latest jobs) can turn out empty: hide the whole block (the standard, with no gap left), show its heading only, or its heading and a message. In the visual builder a hidden one still shows a box to click |
| **Link** | Link the whole block to a page, a part of a page or a web address, optionally in a new tab. Its own buttons and links still go to theirs |
| **Admin label** | Your own name for the block in the builder. Visitors never see it |

### Design

| Toggle | Settings |
|---|---|
| **Small heading** | For blocks with a small heading (eyebrow): a decoration (a line on both sides, a line before it, or a dot) with its colour and line length, a pill's colour, and the same text settings as Heading text (font, size, colour, capitals, letter spacing, alignment…) |
| **Text** | Heading alignment, heading decoration (site style, none, a short bar under the heading, a full-width line under it, or a short bar above it), heading size and text size |
| **Heading text**, **Body text** | Font (any Branding font), weight, italic, capitals, underline, small capitals, size, line height, letter spacing, alignment, colour and a text shadow. Heading text has tabs: **All** sets every heading, and **H1** to **H6** set one level, which wins over All for that level. They reach headings typed in rich text too (a Text block's h2 and h3), and the block's own heading is an H2. Body text reaches the paragraphs, lists and quotes |
| **Link text** | Colour, colour when pointed at, weight, and underline (always, when pointed at, or never). Buttons keep their own style |
| **Buttons** | The look of all the block's buttons (see [Buttons](#buttons)) |
| **Items** (named for the block: *Cards*, *Numbers*, *Steps*, *People*, *Quotes*, *Questions*, *Images*, *Logos*, *Icon buttons*) | Blocks with repeated items style each one: background colour or gradient (the text turns light on a dark colour), padding, the space between items, corners, border width, line and colour, an accent edge (a thicker line along the top, left, bottom or right), shadow, and **lines between items** instead of gaps. **Columns** sets how many items sit side by side on computers, tablets and phones, each on its own (e.g. 2 on phones for a 2 × 2 grid); blank keeps the block's standard. In the Hover state: background, border colour, shadow and a lift |
| **Icons** / **Step numbers** / **Quote mark** | The items' icon: a background (none, a soft tint, solid or an outline ring), shape (square, rounded or circle), size, colour (and on hover) and the tile's colour |
| **Title text**, **Number text**, **Label text**… | Each text part of the items on its own, with the same settings as Heading text: e.g. stat numbers in gold, or a card title as a small coloured capitals label |
| **Lists & quotes** | Bullets (dots, circles, squares or none), numbers (1, 01, a, A, i, I or none), whether they sit inside or outside the text, the list indent, and a quote's line width and colour |
| **Dividers** | A shape along the top or bottom edge (slant, curve, wave, layered waves, triangle, arrow, mountains or zigzag), with its colour (usually the next block's), height, how often it repeats, flipped, and in front of or behind the content |
| **Sizing** | Content width (narrow, contained, wide or full width), an exact width, max width and how the content lines up, height, min and max height (100 vh fills the screen) and where the content sits in it |
| **Spacing** | Space above and below, **overlap the block above** (from Small to Huge, e.g. a screenshot over a banner), and exact space inside (padding) and outside (margin) for each side, in px, rem or %. Margin can be below 0, so blocks overlap. Every size takes decimals (1.1 rem, -0.025 em); the ↑ and ↓ keys step by a whole number, or 0.05 for a line height and 0.1 for letter spacing |
| **Border** | A border line (none, solid, dashed, dotted, double, groove, ridge, inset or outset), width, colour and sides; a width and a colour for each side; and a rounded size for each corner |
| **Box shadow** | None, *Site style* (from Branding), Soft, Medium, Strong, Inner soft, Inner strong, or **Custom**: how far it falls across and down, the blur, the spread, the colour, its strength, and inside or outside |
| **Filters** | For the pictures in the block: greyscale, brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, opacity, hue shift, invert and sepia, optionally back to full colour on hover; and how the whole block blends with what's behind it (multiply, screen, overlay…) |
| **Transform** | Scale across and down, rotate, skew, move across and down (each move can differ on tablets and phones), and the point it transforms from |
| **Animation** | The whole block, or **each item** one after another (its cards, features, testimonials, steps…). An entrance animation as the block comes into view (once it's a fifth of the way up the window) (fade, slide up, down, left or right, zoom, bounce, flip, fold or roll), its speed or exact duration, a delay (e.g. to bring cards in one after another), intensity, starting opacity, speed curve, and repeat for ever. None for visitors who prefer less motion |

On a block, the border and shadow go round its panel when it has one, and round the whole block otherwise. *Automatic* gives a panel the site's shadow and leaves other blocks without one. Images (in Image, Image + text, Image cards and Gallery) have their own corners, border and shadow in their **Image** toggle. Features and Image cards keep **Card layout** (Image cards: **Images & card layout**) for whether each card is a box and where its icon sits; their colours, shadow, icon and text are under the Items toggles. Steps have a **Connectors** toggle (a solid, dashed or dotted line joining the step numbers across each row, and its colour), and each step can be marked **Highlight this step**. Buttons with custom colours can have a border line and width, and any button can have a shadow.

**Hard-to-read colours.** A text colour warns under its field when it would be hard to read on the colour behind it: an item's text on the items' background, an icon on its solid tile, the small heading on its pill, and Heading text or Body text on the block's own custom background colour. It's a warning only; the colour still saves.

**Different on tablets and phones.** Sizes and spacing can have their own value on each screen size. Use the desktop, tablet and phone buttons beside the setting; in the visual builder they follow the Phone, Tablet and Desktop tabs. A dot marks a screen size with its own value, and an empty one shows the value it uses. A desktop value applies on every screen unless tablets have their own; a tablet value also applies on phones unless phones have their own.

## Small headings

Blocks with a heading (and Text, which has none of its own) can show a **Small heading** above it, such as *A solid SaaS* or *Most popular*. It **looks like** small capitals in the site colour, or a **pill** (a badge). On a dark background it turns light.

## Buttons

Buttons appear in many blocks. On the **Content** tab, each one has a **label**, a **link** (a full web address, a page on this site starting with `/`, a part of the page starting with `#`, an email address or a phone number), an optional [icon](#icons) and whether it **opens in a new tab**.

Their look is on the **Design** tab, under **Buttons**, for all the block's buttons: a **style**, a **width**, and a **shadow**. **Style** is one of the four button styles from Branding, *Automatic* (the right style for the background the buttons sit on), or **custom colours** (background, text, colours on hover, and a border). Leave the background blank for a see-through **outline button**, e.g. white text and border on a banner.

For a button that should look different, such as a plainer second button, turn on **This button looks different** under it and give it its own look. Buttons saved before this keep the look they had.

## Icons

Features, steps, stats, image cards and buttons can show an icon. Type to search over 1,600 icons using everyday words, such as *calendar*, *map* or *trophy*. Add *filled* or *logo* to find those styles. Icons are stored on the site, so nothing is loaded from another server.

## Videos, maps and privacy

Until a visitor accepts marketing cookies, a video shows a still picture with a play button, and nothing loads from YouTube, Vimeo or Adilo until it's clicked. Once marketing cookies are allowed, the player shows straight away. YouTube videos always use its privacy-enhanced mode.

SoundCloud and Spotify players in an Audio block work the same way: a play button with the title until marketing cookies are allowed, and nothing loads from them until it's clicked.

Maps work the same way: until marketing cookies are allowed, a map shows a placeholder with the address, **Show map** and **Open in Google Maps**, and nothing loads from Google until one is clicked. Only the address is sent to Google.

## Images

Images can be JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or SVG, up to 2 MB, as standard; [Website › Media › Images](media.md#images) changes both, and can shrink large photos and remove their location data as they're uploaded. An SVG stays sharp at every size, which suits logos and icons. It must be a plain drawing: one with scripts, links to other files or other active content is refused when you upload it, because uploads are served from the site's own address. If yours is refused, export it again as a plain SVG (most design apps have an option for this), or use a PNG or WebP. A page's **Share image** can't be an SVG, because social sites don't show them. An Image or Image + text block needs **Alternative text**: a short description of the picture for people using screen readers, and for search engines.

Pages can also be built from a JSON file with `php artisan site-builder:page`, checked as the editor checks them: see [Building pages from JSON](developers/extending.md#building-pages-from-json).

To use an image that's already uploaded, click **Choose from library** beside any image field. It shows the [media library](media.md#library) as pictures, newest first: type part of a name to search, filter to images or SVG drawings, and **Show more** for older ones. Click one, then **Use this file**. Or upload a new one there and then. The same file is used again, not copied. If the library has alt text for the image and the block's **Alternative text** is blank, it's filled in for you. A share image's list leaves out SVGs. The Audio block's sound file has **Choose from library** too.

Every image you upload goes into the [media library](media.md#library), and stays there when you remove it from a page, so you can use it again. Delete files you no longer need there; one that a page, library block or template uses can't be deleted.

## Duplicating, deleting and restoring pages

**Duplicate** (in the list of pages) makes a draft copy, titled *… (copy)*, with its own address. It never replaces a built-in page until you choose one.

**Delete** moves a page to the bin. It stops showing on the site, and any built-in page it replaced shows again. A page in the bin can still be edited, but its status and the built-in page it replaces can't be changed until it's restored. Use the list's **Deleted records** filter to find it, then **Restore** it or **Delete permanently**. Deleting permanently also removes its revisions and any images nothing else uses, and can't be undone.
