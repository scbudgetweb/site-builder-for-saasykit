# Revisions

**Where:** Admin › Website › Pages › (a page) › **Revisions**, at the top of the page editor
**Who can use it:** anyone who can edit pages

Every time a page is saved with changes, the site keeps a copy of that version: a **revision**. If something goes wrong, you can look at an earlier version and put it back.

## What a revision keeps

A revision keeps the page's **title**, its **blocks**, whether it shows the **standard page banner**, and its **search & sharing settings** (search title, description, share image and *Hide from search engines*). It also records **who saved it** and **when**.

It doesn't keep the page's address (URL), its status (draft or published) or which built-in page it replaces. Restoring a revision never changes those.

A revision is only made when one of the kept settings changes. Publishing a page, or saving without changes, doesn't add one.

The **last 30** revisions of each page are kept; older ones are removed automatically. A developer can change the number.

## Looking at earlier versions

1. Open the page in the editor and click **Revisions** at the top.
2. A panel lists the versions, newest first, with the date and time, who saved it and the page title. The newest is marked **Current**.
3. Click **Preview** to open that version in a new tab. A bar at the top says it's an earlier version and isn't live. The preview link works for an hour.

## Restoring a version

1. In **Revisions**, click **Restore** next to the version you want.
2. The panel closes and that version is loaded into the editor. **Nothing has changed on the site yet.** A message tells you which version you're looking at.
3. Check it, then click **Save** to make it live. Saving makes a new revision, so the version you replaced is still in the list if you change your mind.

To discard a restored version instead, leave the page without saving.

## Images

Images used by a kept revision stay in storage, so restoring an older version never brings back a missing picture. Once no page, library block or revision uses an image any more, it's deleted.

## Deleting a page

A page in the bin keeps its revisions. Deleting a page permanently also deletes its revisions.

The [block library](block-library.md) doesn't keep revisions.
